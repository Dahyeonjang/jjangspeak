Part 1 — 문법 (Grammar)
1. 빈칸에 알맞은 단어를 고르세요.
She ______ to the gym every morning.
If I ______ you, I would take the job.
They ______ never been to Paris before.
2. 수의 일치 — 알맞은 것을 고르세요.
Either my parents or my sister ______ coming to the party.
Everyone ______ that he is a great musician.
3. to 부정사 — 알맞은 것을 고르세요.
I want ______ how to cook Italian food.
He decided ______ abroad for further studies.
4. 문장 형식 — 몇 형식인지 고르세요.
I bought a new laptop.
They gave me a wonderful gift.
She looks happy today.
We consider him a great leader.
5. 문장 변환 — 빈칸을 채우세요.
He gave a book to me. → (4형식으로)
She told me a story. → (3형식으로)
We elected him the president. → (3형식으로)
6. 틀린 부분을 고쳐 쓰세요.
