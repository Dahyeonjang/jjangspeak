Part 1 — 읽기
짧은 글을 읽고 답해 주세요. 한국어로 답하셔도 괜찮아요.
1. 다음 글을 읽고 질문에 답하세요.
Tom enjoys cooking during his free time. He often tries new recipes from different countries. Last week, he made sushi for the first time and shared it with his friends. They all loved it and asked him to cook it again.
What does Tom like to do in his free time?
What did Tom cook last week?
How did Tom's friends react to the sushi?
2. 빈칸을 채우세요.
Jenny loves reading books. Her favorite genres are mystery and fantasy. Every month, she visits the library to borrow new books. Yesterday, she found a book called "The Lost Island," which she started reading immediately.
Jenny's favorite genres are and .
Jenny visits the library every .
The book she found is titled "".
3. 광고를 읽고 답하세요.
Looking for fresh fruits and vegetables? Visit Green Market, where everything is organic and locally sourced. Open daily from 8 AM to 8 PM. Find us at 123 Main Street!
What does Green Market sell?
What time does Green Market close?
4. 조금 긴 글이에요.
James works as a software engineer in a large company. He starts his day at 9 AM and usually finishes work by 6 PM. Although his job is challenging, he enjoys solving problems and learning new things. On weekends, he spends time with his family or plays video games to relax.
What is James's job?
What does James do to relax on weekends?

Part 3 — 문장 만들기
1. 어색한 곳이 하나씩 있어요. 바르게 다시 써 주세요.
He don't like coffee.
She always make me laugh with her jokes.
The restaurant is very expensiver than I expected.
I want go home now.
2. 영어로 옮겨 주세요.
오늘 날씨가 정말 좋네요.
이 근처에 좋은 카페를 추천해 주시겠어요?
저는 매운 음식을 잘 먹지 못해요.
지금 가야 할 시간이 되었어요.
Part 4 — 한 문장 키우기
한 문장이 조금씩 길어져요. 앞에서 쓴 문장을 그대로 가져와서 붙여 가시면 돼요.
영어 단어가 안 떠오르면 그 자리만 한국어로 두셔도 괜찮아요.
1. 저는 주말마다 친구를 만나요.
2. 저는 주말마다 친구를 만나는 걸 좋아해요.
3. 저는 시간이 날 때 주말마다 친구를 만나는 걸 좋아해요.
